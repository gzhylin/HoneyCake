About

Ukrainian recipes, baked in Texas

Honey Cake is a small home bakery run by Oksana in the Austin area.

She bakes traditional Ukrainian cakes and pastries, starting with the one the bakery is named after: a tender, many-layered honey cake. Every order is baked from scratch in small batches, for birthdays, holidays, weddings and everyday treats.

  • Baked fresh

    Prepared within 24 hours before your pickup.

  • No preservatives

    Made with high-quality ingredients and nothing added to extend shelf life.

  • Made to order

    Every cake is baked for your order and your occasion.

Good to know

  • Pickup

    Leander, TX, about 30 minutes from downtown Austin. Pickup times are flexible.

  • Delivery

    Delivery is available for certain orders. Please ask when ordering.

  • Payment

    Cash, Zelle or Venmo. Checks are not accepted.

  • Freshness

    Baked within 24 hours before pickup, with no preservatives. Refrigerate within 30 minutes of pickup and enjoy within 72 hours.

  • Dietary needs

    Sugar-free and gluten-free options are not available at the moment.
